How points are calculated

100k a day, by share
100,000 points a day, paid out hourly, split by your share of all enrolled LP.
Lowest sample counts
Balances are sampled about every 15 minutes. Your lowest sample in the hour is what counts. Add or remove between samples and it may be missed.
Withdraw, stop earning
LP gone at a sample, that hour pays nothing. Add it back and points resume.
No lockup
Amount only. No boost, no lock, no deposit. Your LP stays in Uniswap.
Regular wallets only
Multisig and smart-contract wallets can't sign here. Enroll from the wallet that holds the LP.
One points balance
LP points and staking points are the same STERLING points. This page shows the LP side only. What points redeem for is announced separately; they are not a promise of value.
Sterling LP Rewards runs alongside OKX's official Earn campaign for the Uniswap V2 STERLING/wNVDAx pool on X Layer. Enrollment is a signature only — Sterling is non-custodial and never touches your LP or funds. STERLING points are a loyalty measure, not a security, financial instrument, or promise of value; eligibility may exclude restricted regions.
