Sterling Yield Terminal is a non-custodial research tool — it ranks routes and links out to each protocol's own app, where you review and sign yourself. It never custodies funds, never signs, and never trades on your behalf; the deposit link opens the protocol's own interface in a new tab. Data from DefiLlama (industry-standard on-chain aggregator); only mature audited protocols (Aave / Compound / Morpho / Sky / Spark / Fluid / Venus) above liquidity floors are ranked — the allowlist + TVL floor is a filter, not a risk score, and no token contract is identity-verified (risk_scored: false). Yields are historical/current observations, not a promise of returns; 30-day mean is shown to flag transient spikes; base vs reward split shows how much is base interest vs decaying incentives. Cross-chain APY excludes bridge cost. Not for US / restricted-region users. Not investment advice. Prototype · engine v0.1.
